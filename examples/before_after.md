# Before / After: Agency Website Copy

This example shows the kind of draft `depattern` is built to catch.

## Before

Furthermore, in today's fast-moving digital ecosystem, small businesses must leverage cutting-edge solutions to unlock their full potential. It is important to note that a website is no longer just a website. It is a seamless hub that elevates customer engagement, optimizes workflows, and creates a transformative journey for every visitor.

In conclusion, businesses that fail to embrace this paradigm shift will be left behind by competitors who understand the power of innovation.

## depattern flags

```text
Pattern Artifact Score: 6/9

- Banned transitions: furthermore, it is important to note, in conclusion
- Abstract nouns: ecosystem, solutions, optimization, innovation
- Vague language: cutting-edge, seamless, transformative journey
- Rhythm issue: sentences have similar length and cadence
```

## After

Most small businesses do not need a bigger website. They need a front door that answers the buyer's question, proves the business is active, and makes the next step obvious.

That usually means three things: a clean service page, a review/request path, and a follow-up system for people who are interested but not ready to call yet.

## Why the rewrite works

- It starts with the real buyer problem.
- It names the actual parts of the system.
- It removes decorative language.
- It gives the reader something concrete to evaluate.
